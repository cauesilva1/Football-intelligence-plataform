OmniScout Insights is the reading room for a biweekly sports-analytics newsletter. Email stays on Substack. Each edition picks one scouting question and answers only that — with the data we actually hold, and one chart.

## One question per edition

No roundup, and no invented percentiles. A sporting director should be able to repeat the question after the first paragraph. The sample floor stays visible when the numbers are thin.

## What an edition holds

- The question, stated once.
- The data behind the answer, with its limits.
- One chart that carries the argument.
